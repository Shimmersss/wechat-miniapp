# 10 出借与借入

涉及接口：`GET /loans/options`、`POST /loans`、`GET /loans`、`GET /loans/:id`、`POST /loans/:id/return`、`POST /loans/:id/recheck`
涉及页面：`staff/loans/index|detail|edit`、库存详情借还入口

单据状态：active（展示时逾期为 overdue）、returned_recheck、closed。设备：出借 in_stock→loaned_out→recheck→in_stock；借入 borrowed_in→returned。首期不收押金、逾期费、赔偿。
